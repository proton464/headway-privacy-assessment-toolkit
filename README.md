# Product / AI / Vendor Privacy Assessment Toolkit

**Portfolio Project** | Demonstrates consistent privacy reviews of product features, AI use cases, and vendors involving PHI, plus BAA inventory management.

## One-Sentence Elevator Pitch
A lightweight privacy assessment toolkit and BAA inventory that shows how to conduct consistent, documented reviews of products, AI use cases, and vendors.

## Mapping to Headway Privacy Manager JD
| JD Requirement | How This Project Evidences It |
|---------------|--------------------------------|
| Conduct privacy assessments for products, vendors, workflows, and AI/ML use cases involving PHI | Assessment Checklist + Sample Assessments |
| Partner with the AI Governance Program on data use, minimum necessary, retention, and human oversight | AI-specific questions and sample AI assessment |
| Conduct privacy diligence on vendors and maintain the BAA inventory | BAA Vendor Inventory |
| Build practical checklists and playbooks | Checklist + Escalation Playbook |

## Excel Workbook
`Headway_Privacy_Assessment_Toolkit.xlsx` includes a reusable checklist, sample completed assessments, BAA/vendor inventory with gaps, and an escalation playbook. Blank rows are provided for additions.

## Strongest Interview Talking Point
“This toolkit shows how I would make privacy reviews of products, AI use cases, and vendors consistent, documented, and scalable.”
